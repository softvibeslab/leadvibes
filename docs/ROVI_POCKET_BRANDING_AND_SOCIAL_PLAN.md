# ROVI Pocket - Branding and Social Media Rollout Plan

Cutoff date: April 8, 2026
Target horizon: May 29, 2026
Primary objective: define the brand system and leave the social media presence of `ROVI Pocket` fully "encamizada" and launch-ready before the webinar growth push.

## Executive summary

`ROVI Pocket` already has product narrative, MVP focus, and webinar launch planning. The gap now is not strategy alone; it is brand execution consistency.

The goal of this project is to move from "good product story" to "recognizable brand with social surfaces ready to convert."

That means delivering:

- a clear brand position,
- a visual system that matches the product promise,
- social profiles with coherent bios, avatars, covers and CTA structure,
- reusable templates for content and campaign assets,
- a launch-ready starter pack for organic and webinar promotion.

## What "encamizado de redes" means in this plan

For this project, "encamizado" means applying the brand coherently across the most important social and communication surfaces:

- profile picture / avatar,
- cover or banner assets,
- bios and CTA copy,
- link hub and contact routing,
- pinned assets / highlights / featured sections,
- visual templates for ongoing content,
- launch kit assets for webinar and conversion.

## Assumed priority channels

Unless the team decides otherwise, this plan prioritizes:

- Instagram
- LinkedIn
- Facebook
- WhatsApp Business
- YouTube / Shorts

## Business objective

Use branding and social setup to make `ROVI Pocket` feel credible, differentiated and conversion-ready for brokers who discover the product through content, webinar invites or direct outreach.

## Desired outcome

By the end of this project, the team should have:

- a brand system approved,
- a messaging house aligned with the product,
- social profiles visually aligned and operational,
- a template library for posts, stories, reels and webinar promos,
- a first content starter pack ready for publishing,
- governance rules so the brand stays consistent after launch.

## Recommended scope

### Included

- messaging house,
- visual identity base,
- avatar and logo lockups,
- color and type rules,
- social media profile setup,
- covers and banners,
- profile bios and CTA copy,
- highlight / featured / pinned structure,
- template system for posts and stories,
- webinar promo kit,
- first starter pack of launch content,
- brand manual lite and ops handoff.

### Out of scope unless extra capacity appears

- full website redesign,
- long-form video series production,
- paid media creative expansion beyond core launch kit,
- advanced photography production,
- full influencer or ambassador program.

## Main risks

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|--------|------------|
| Branding becomes too abstract and detached from product | High | Keep product promise and broker pain at the center of every decision |
| Social setup happens before message is locked | High | Freeze positioning and CTA before full encamizado |
| Too many channels dilute quality | Medium | Prioritize 5 channels max for phase 1 |
| Templates look generic and do not differentiate Pocket | High | Use a strong visual direction tied to mobile-first momentum and clarity |
| No governance after launch | Medium | Ship brand ops, naming rules and approval flow with the assets |

## Critical path

Recommended sequence:

1. `PKBR-001` Define brand strategy and messaging house.
2. `PKBR-002` Build visual identity system.
3. `PKBR-003` Define social architecture and channel priorities.
4. `PKBR-004` Encamizar core social profiles.
5. `PKBR-005` Create content template system.
6. `PKBR-007` Prepare launch starter pack.
7. `PKBR-008` Prepare webinar and campaign brand kit.
8. `PKBR-010` Run QA, go-live and first optimization loop.

`PKBR-006` and `PKBR-009` can run in parallel if they do not block the chain above.

## Backlog summary

### `PKBR-001` Define brand strategy and positioning

Area owner: Brand Strategy

Subtasks:

- align ICP, broker pain and brand personality,
- define promise, tagline and messaging house,
- approve tone of voice, claims and forbidden language.

### `PKBR-002` Build visual identity system

Area owner: Brand Design

Subtasks:

- design avatar, logo lockups and marks,
- define color, typography and composition rules,
- approve moodboard and visual direction.

### `PKBR-003` Define social architecture and channel setup

Area owner: Social Media

Subtasks:

- prioritize channels and objective by network,
- resolve handles, bios, links and CTA routing,
- define highlight, featured and pinned structure.

### `PKBR-004` Encamizar social profiles

Area owner: Brand Design + Social Media

Subtasks:

- create avatars, covers and banners by channel,
- configure bios, links and contact buttons,
- load highlights, pinned assets and featured sections.

### `PKBR-005` Create content template system

Area owner: Brand Design

Subtasks:

- create carousel and feed templates,
- create story and reel templates,
- create webinar promo templates and CTA frames.

### `PKBR-006` Build branded asset pack

Area owner: Motion / Creative

Subtasks:

- create icons, stickers and UI-brand supporting assets,
- create logo motion intro / outro and watermark kit,
- define art direction references for future shoots.

### `PKBR-007` Prepare launch starter pack

Area owner: Content + Social Media

Subtasks:

- define first 12-15 posts,
- produce launch captions and content copy,
- align publish order with webinar and product narrative.

### `PKBR-008` Prepare webinar brand kit

Area owner: Product Marketing + Design

Subtasks:

- create webinar visual key,
- prepare speaker deck master and promo visuals,
- prepare countdown, replay and CTA assets.

### `PKBR-009` Establish governance and brand ops

Area owner: Marketing Ops

Subtasks:

- publish brand manual lite,
- define file structure and naming conventions,
- define approval flow and asset handoff.

### `PKBR-010` QA, go-live and optimization

Area owner: Social Media + Marketing Ops

Subtasks:

- run cross-platform profile QA,
- publish profiles and first asset wave,
- review early metrics and create iteration backlog.

## Decisions recommended today

1. Treat branding as an operational layer of launch, not just a design exercise.
2. Keep the brand centered on brokers, mobile clarity and daily momentum.
3. Prioritize profile readiness and template systems over "big campaign" experimentation.
4. Align every social surface with the webinar CTA and launch path.
